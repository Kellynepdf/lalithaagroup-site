ABOUT US

Company Overview: Lalithaa Group, officially known as Lalithaa Edible Oil Fats India Private Limited, is a Hyderabad-based company committed to delivering high-quality edible oils and spices. We have grown into a trusted name, offering authentic and flavourful spices to meet diverse customer needs.


Quality & Infrastructure: Quality is at the heart of everything we do. Our products meet strict industry standards and are backed by recognized quality certifications. With state-of-the-art infrastructure and a strong supply chain, we maintain our commitment to innovation and excellence.



Import & Export Specialization: In addition to FMCG, we specialize in importing high-quality sunflower and palm oil. While we do not manufacture these oils, we ensure their premium quality and reliable supply.


Logistics & Business Terms: We offer packaging in 1L pouches, 5L cans, and 18L cans. Delivery lead times are 30-35 days for initial orders and 20-25 days for repeats. We follow a 100% advance payment policy and provide shipping options including CIF, CIP, and DAP Incoterms.


Vision & Thanks: As we grow, we aim to expand into a wider range of FMCG products. We thank you for being a part of the Lalithaa Group family. Experience the authentic taste of India—where quality meets tradition.
